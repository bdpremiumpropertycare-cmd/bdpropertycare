Our Story

BD Premium Property Care was established to provide dependable exterior property maintenance services to homeowners and businesses throughout County Cork.

Ireland's weather can take a toll on driveways, patios, gutters, windows and outdoor spaces. Our goal is to help property owners keep their homes and commercial premises clean, presentable and well maintained throughout the year.

As a growing local business, we focus on delivering professional workmanship, clear communication, reliable service and long-term customer satisfaction.
