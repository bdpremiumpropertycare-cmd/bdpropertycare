When you reach out to us through our website forms or Google Forms to get a price estimate, we collect: your full name, your property address or Eircode, your mobile phone number, and your email address.

We use your details strictly to get in touch with you, look at your property needs, and provide an accurate price quote for our cleaning and maintenance services. To run our booking and inquiry system smoothly, your information passes through secure digital tools we use, including Formspree and Google Forms. We do not otherwise share your information with third parties.

5. How Long We Keep Your Data

If you just ask for a quote and do not proceed with our services, we delete your personal information after 12 months. If you hire us for a job, we keep your records safely for as required by Irish tax and business laws.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you are always in control of your personal information. You can ask us at any time to: see what information we have about you (access), correct any mistakes in your details (rectification), delete your information (erasure), restrict how we use it, object to our processing, or receive a copy in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at info@bdpropertycare.ie, and we will sort it out for you within 48 hours.

7. Automated Decision-Making

We do not use automated decision-making or profiling based on your personal information. All price estimates and business decisions are reviewed by a person.

8. Making a Complaint

If you ever feel we haven't handled your data properly, you have the right to contact the Irish Data Protection Commission (DPC) via their website at dataprotection.ie.
