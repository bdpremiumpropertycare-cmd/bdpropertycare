Safety & Technical Highlights

Pressure matched to the surface

Block paving, concrete, natural stone and tarmac tolerate different pressures. We set the pump and nozzle for the surface in front of us, not one setting for everything.

Runoff is managed

Washing carries dirt, moss and oil into the runoff. We direct it away from borders, drains and neighbouring property wherever the site allows.

Right method, every time

If a surface is delicate or deteriorating we will recommend soft washing or steam cleaning instead of risking damage.

When We Choose Steam Cleaning Over Pressure Washing

Pressure washing is the right tool for tough, durable surfaces. Steam cleaning is our choice when force itself is the risk.

  • Delicate natural stone such as limestone and sandstone, where high pressure can pit or erode the face.
  • Older masonry and pointing that may wash out under a pressure lance.
  • Decorative or heritage finishes where the original surface needs protecting.
  • Localised grease, gum and stains that respond to heat better than to force.

We assess the surface before we start and tell you which method we recommend. Read more about steam cleaning.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will pressure washing damage my driveway or patio?

Not when the pressure and nozzle are matched to the surface. We assess the material first, and if it is delicate or deteriorating we will recommend soft washing or steam cleaning instead. Loose pointing or cracked paving can show up once the dirt is gone, and we will point it out.

Is pressure washing a good time to seal paving?

Yes. A freshly cleaned surface is the right starting point for sealant. Read our paving sealant article and mention it when you request your quote.

What happens if the weather is bad on the day?

We reserve the right to reschedule around severe weather rather than compromise on quality, and we will agree a new date with you.
