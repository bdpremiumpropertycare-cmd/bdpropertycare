Steam Cleaning in Cork

Steam cleaning uses heat with low-volume, low-pressure delivery to lift grime, grease and organic growth from delicate surfaces. It is our choice where high pressure could pit stone, wash out pointing or damage a finish.

Safety & Technical Highlights

Heat, not force

Steam loosens dirt and staining with heat, so we do not need aggressive pressure that can erode soft stone or open up joints.

Surface-first decision

We assess the material before choosing steam, soft washing or pressure washing, and explain why we recommend it.

Safe working area

Hot steam is hazardous, so we keep children, pets and passers-by clear of the work area while equipment is running.

Pressure Washing Suits

  • Tarmac, concrete and sound block paving
  • Heavy moss and weed growth
  • Large driveways and commercial facades

Steam Cleaning Suits

  • Delicate natural stone and old masonry
  • Pointing that could wash out
  • Decorative surfaces and spot stains

Frequently Asked Questions

When do you use steam instead of a pressure washer?

When force is the risk: delicate natural stone, older masonry and pointing, and decorative finishes. For tougher surfaces, pressure washing is usually the better tool.

Can steam damage my surface?

Steam is gentler than high pressure, but every surface is different. We assess the material first and flag anything fragile before we start.

Does steam cleaning use chemicals?

Steam cleans mainly with heat. If we think a treatment would help with biological growth, we will tell you at quote stage so you can decide.
